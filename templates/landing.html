Anonymous reporting

Hide your name from staff when you report sensitive matters. You can still track the case.

Frequently asked questions

Who can see my complaint?

You and the one office it is assigned to. Staff in other offices and system administrators cannot open it.

Can I report without showing my name?

Yes. Tick "Hide my name from staff" when you submit. You still need an account so you can follow progress.

How long will I wait for an answer?

Each complaint has a response deadline based on its priority: 24 hours for urgent, 48 hours for high, and a few days for normal complaints.

What if I am not happy with the outcome?

Reopen the complaint, or ask the office to send it to the Dean of Student Affairs.
